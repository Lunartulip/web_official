Who pays for the next stage of AI infrastructure after NVIDIA’s leadership transition?

NVIDIA guided to $130bn next quarter, while management raised its FY2028 revenue-growth outlook to 75%. We trace customer funding into equipment purchases and hosting cash flows, then examine how HBM inventory capital and CPO adoption change the supplier picture.

Using AlphaMap relationships and original calculations, LunarTulip Research 01 compares current revenue concentration with future capacity, and shows why buyers’ financing calendars matter for distant orders.
